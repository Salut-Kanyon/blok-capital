Value-Add • Noe Valley, San Francisco

24th Street — Noe Valley

Repositioning and co-living conversion in San Francisco's vibrant Noe Valley neighborhood

6Units
18Bedrooms
Co-livingStrategy

The Opportunity

This Noe Valley property represented a classic Blok Capital opportunity: an under-managed building with outdated units and below-market rents in a high-demand neighborhood. The property required comprehensive renovation to bring units to market standards and optimize floor plans for maximum bedroom count.

Our Approach

Blok Capital acquired the property and executed a full repositioning strategy. Our in-house team renovated all units with modern finishes, updated kitchens and bathrooms, and reconfigured layouts to create 18 bedrooms across 6 co-living units. All construction was managed internally, achieving significant cost savings versus market contractor rates.

Scope of Work

  • Full unit renovations
  • Kitchen and bathroom upgrades
  • Co-living conversion (18 bedrooms)
  • Floor plan optimization
  • Electrical system updates
  • Common area improvements

The Results

The renovated property now offers 18 high-quality bedrooms at market rents, significantly outperforming pre-renovation income. The Noe Valley location ensures strong tenant demand and low vacancy. This project exemplifies Blok Capital's repeatable value-add playbook.
