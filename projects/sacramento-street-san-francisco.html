The Opportunity

Located on prestigious Sacramento Street, this 7-unit building had excellent bones but suffered from years of deferred maintenance and outdated unit configurations. The property's prime location warranted a premium renovation approach.

Our Approach

Blok Capital acquired the property and executed an elevated renovation strategy befitting the Sacramento Street address. Each of the 7 units received high-quality kitchen and bathroom upgrades, refinished hardwood floors, and thoughtful layout improvements to maximize bedroom count.

Scope of Work

  • Premium unit renovations
  • High-end kitchen finishes
  • Modern bathroom upgrades
  • Hardwood floor refinishing
  • Layout optimization
  • Common area modernization

The Results

The repositioned property now commands premium rents reflecting its desirable location and quality finishes. With 14 bedrooms across 7 units, the building achieves strong income performance and attracts stable, long-term residents.
