Oak Street — Lower Haight

Boutique multifamily repositioning in San Francisco's eclectic Lower Haight

4Units
20Bedrooms
Value-AddStrategy

The Opportunity

This Lower Haight property offered a prime location at the crossroads of several sought-after neighborhoods, but the 4-unit building had been neglected for years. Outdated unit configurations and deferred maintenance resulted in below-market rents despite strong neighborhood demand.

Our Approach

Blok Capital acquired the building and implemented a thorough renovation across all 4 units. Our in-house team reconfigured layouts to maximize bedroom count, installed modern kitchens and bathrooms, and refreshed common areas—achieving significant cost savings through our vertically integrated approach.

Scope of Work

  • Full unit renovations
  • Kitchen and bathroom upgrades
  • Floor plan reconfiguration
  • Common area modernization
  • Building systems updates
  • Exterior improvements

The Results

The fully renovated building now offers 20 high-quality bedrooms at market rents, significantly outperforming pre-renovation income. The Lower Haight's central location and vibrant community ensure strong tenant demand and low vacancy rates.
