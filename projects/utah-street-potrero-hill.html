Value-Add • Potrero Hill, San Francisco

Utah Street — Potrero Hill

Value-add multifamily acquisition in San Francisco's sunny Potrero Hill neighborhood

5Units
15Bedrooms
Value-AddStrategy

The Opportunity

This Potrero Hill property was a classic under-managed building in one of San Francisco's most desirable neighborhoods. With its sunny microclimate and proximity to the tech corridor, the location warranted premium rents—but dated finishes and deferred maintenance held the property well below its potential.

Our Approach

Blok Capital acquired the 5-unit building and executed a comprehensive renovation program. Our in-house team modernized all units with contemporary finishes, updated kitchens and bathrooms, and optimized floor plans to create 15 well-appointed bedrooms—all at approximately 35% below market contractor costs.

Scope of Work

  • Full unit renovations
  • Kitchen and bathroom modernization
  • Floor plan optimization
  • Electrical and plumbing upgrades
  • Common area improvements
  • Exterior maintenance

The Results

The repositioned property now commands top-of-market rents reflecting the Potrero Hill address and quality finishes. Strong demand from tech professionals working in the nearby SOMA and Mission Bay corridors ensures consistently high occupancy and stable income.
