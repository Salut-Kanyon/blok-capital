Clay Street — Pacific Heights

Trust sale acquisition and repositioning in a premier San Francisco neighborhood

8Units
16Bedrooms
Trust SaleStrategy

The Opportunity

This Pacific Heights property became available through a trust sale—a common source of Blok Capital acquisitions where estate situations create motivated sellers. The building had been held by the same family for decades, resulting in deferred maintenance and below-market rents despite the premier location.

Our Approach

Blok Capital moved quickly to acquire the property off-market before it reached broader distribution. Our in-house team executed a premium renovation befitting the Pacific Heights location: high-end kitchen finishes, modern bathroom fixtures, refinished hardwood floors, and updated common areas. The 8-unit building was optimized to 16 bedrooms.

Scope of Work

  • Premium kitchen renovations
  • High-end bathroom upgrades
  • Hardwood floor refinishing
  • Common area modernization
  • Building systems updates
  • Exterior improvements

The Results

The renovated property now achieves rents at the top of the Pacific Heights market, reflecting its premium finishes and prime location. The trust sale acquisition allowed Blok Capital to secure the property at favorable pricing, generating strong returns for investors.
