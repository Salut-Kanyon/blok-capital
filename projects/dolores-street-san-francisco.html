Iconic Mission District multifamily transformed through strategic renovation

7Units

Value-AddStrategy

The Opportunity

This Dolores Street building offered an exceptional location in the heart of the Mission District, steps from Dolores Park and surrounded by vibrant retail and dining. However, the property had been under-managed with outdated units that failed to capture the neighborhood's rental premiums.

Our Approach

Blok Capital acquired the property and implemented a thorough renovation program across all 7 units. Our in-house team modernized kitchens and bathrooms, refinished original hardwood floors, and optimized layouts to create 14 well-configured bedrooms—all at approximately 35% below market contractor costs.

Scope of Work

  • Full unit renovations
  • Kitchen and bathroom modernization
  • Hardwood floor refinishing
  • Floor plan optimization
  • Common area upgrades
  • Building systems updates

The Results

The fully renovated building now achieves premium rents reflecting the Dolores Street address and proximity to Dolores Park. Strong tenant demand in the Mission District ensures consistently high occupancy and stable cash flow for investors.
