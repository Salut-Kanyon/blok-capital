The asset class

What is IOS?

Industrial Outdoor Storage (IOS) refers to industrial sites used primarily for outdoor uses—truck and trailer parking, container storage, equipment yards, contractor yards, and building-materials staging. These properties serve the logistics, construction, and industrial-services tenants that keep supply chains moving.

IOS is structurally scarce. Industrial zoning that permits outdoor storage is increasingly difficult to entitle, and infill parcels near major infrastructure cannot be recreated. That scarcity, combined with strong tenant demand and NNN lease structures, drives durable cash flow and long-term value.

Blok Capital sources, acquires, improves, and operates IOS assets as a vertically integrated owner-operator and co-GP—bringing the same in-house discipline that powers our multifamily platform to a complementary, supply-constrained asset class.

  • Acquisitions

    Sourcing 3+ acre Industrial Outdoor Storage sites in top US MSAs, near major highways, ports, and rail infrastructure.

  • Entitlements & Zoning

    We target industrial zoning with outdoor storage as a permitted use—rights that are difficult to recreate and create durable value.

  • Site Development & Renovation

    Yard improvements, paving, fencing, lighting, drainage, and grade-level access executed with our in-house construction discipline.

  • Leasing & Tenant Mix

    Truck and trailer logistics, container storage, equipment rental, building materials, and waste & environmental services.

  • Asset & Property Management

    NNN lease administration, annual escalations, compliance, and hands-on operations through our integrated platform.
