Property Management

San Francisco multifamily operations

Leasing, care, and the books — handled in house, only in San Francisco.

The work

Full-service property operations.

Blok Capital manages the buildings it owns. Leasing, maintenance, reporting, and San Francisco compliance stay with the same team that bought the asset—no third-party manager between the owner and the building.

That covers 120+ units and $140M of San Francisco multifamily. A manager walks every asset, rents are kept at market, and residents deal with the people who are responsible for the property.

  • Leasing

    Screen tenants, fill vacancies, and keep rents current in San Francisco’s competitive market.

  • Care

    Preventive maintenance and a manager on every asset, so small problems stay small.

  • Reporting

    Rent, expenses, and the books, sent every month to the people who own the building with us.

  • Compliance

    Rent control, fair housing, and the rest of the San Francisco rulebook, handled in house.
