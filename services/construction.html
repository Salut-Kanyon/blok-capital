In-house remodeling with a 35% cost advantage.

Blok Capital renovates with its own crew. Kitchens, bathrooms, floor-plan changes, and the work between tenants are priced inside the firm, about 35% below what the same scope costs on the open market.

Because the people who underwrite the deal also build it, the scope stays tied to the rent. Unit turns, capital items, and the messy middle of a value-add all sit on one schedule.

  • Below market

    About 35% under outside bids, because there is no third-party markup on the work.

  • Unit turns

    Kitchens, baths, floors, and fixtures, finished so the unit can lease again.

  • Floor plans

    Redrawn layouts that add bedrooms, which is where San Francisco rent actually moves.

  • Building systems

    Roof, HVAC, plumbing, and electrical, planned with the renovation instead of after it.
