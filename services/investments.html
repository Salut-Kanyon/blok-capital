Investment Services

Strategic real estate investments

Managed accounts for people who want San Francisco buildings bought, improved, and held.

The offering

Managed accounts for high-net-worth individuals.

Blok Capital runs managed accounts for people who want San Francisco multifamily bought, improved, and held. We focus on 3–10 unit buildings—large enough for professional management, and too small for most institutions.

The playbook is value-add. Distressed and under-managed buildings, trust sales, and tired floor plans. Our in-house construction and property management teams do the work at about 35% below market, then we hold the asset through lease-up and a refinance to a permanent loan.

  • San Francisco only

    3–10 unit buildings in one market we have operated for 8+ years. 120+ units and $140M under management.

  • Value-add

    Distressed assets, trust sales, and underused floor plans, where a renovation changes what the building can earn.

  • In-house edge

    Construction and management sit inside the firm, at about 35% below market, so the margin stays with the investment.

  • Held

    Acquire, improve, lease, and refinance to a permanent loan. Fund and SPV structures for qualified investors.
