Strategy

Our investment approach

Investment Thesis

A repeatable playbook for small multifamily.

We've built a repeatable playbook for San Francisco small multifamily—identifying underperforming assets and transforming them through in-house capabilities.

  • Target Asset Class

    We specialize in 3–10 unit multifamily buildings in San Francisco—a sweet spot underserved by institutional capital but large enough to benefit from professional management.

  • Value-Add Focus

    We target distressed, under-managed properties, trust fund sales, and underutilized floor plans where operational improvements and renovations can unlock significant value.

  • In-House Execution

    Our in-house remodeling and property management teams achieve costs approximately 35% below market, creating a durable competitive advantage.

  • End-to-End Control

    We run the entire process—from planning and design through acquisition, construction, lease-up, stabilization, and refinance to permanent loan—ensuring alignment at every stage.

Expanded Strategy

Industrial Outdoor Storage and entitled industrial land.

Blok Capital is expanding its investment focus to include Industrial Outdoor Storage (IOS) and infill industrial properties with scarce zoning, outdoor storage rights, conditional use permits, grandfathered uses, and other entitlements that are difficult to recreate in today's regulatory environment.

We look for low-coverage industrial parcels, truck and equipment yards, contractor storage sites, construction-material yards, and similar properties where location, zoning, and operational flexibility create durable value.

  • Scarce Industrial Land

    Infill parcels with outdoor storage, yard, or logistics utility in supply-constrained markets.

  • Zoning & Entitlement Value

    Properties with CUPs, grandfathered rights, or approved uses that are difficult to replicate.

  • Operationally Useful Assets

    Sites that support truck parking, contractor yards, materials storage, equipment storage, or fleet operations.

  • Relationship-Driven Sourcing

    Direct owner conversations, broker relationships, and off-market opportunities where nuanced property rights matter.
